Learn More About Us!

Our Mission

At Pacific Trails Resort, our mission is to provide the highest quality camping experience for everyone, prioritizing a safe and welcoming environment. We offer entertainment, calming surroundings, delicious food, and more to ensure every guest enjoys their stay. Our resort offers the perfect blend of calming, natural surroundings and modern convenience. Beyond the peaceful setting, your stay includes memorable entertainment and experiences, from guided nature walks and stargazing sessions to evening campfires. Also, no camping trip is complete without incredible food! We are proud to offer a destination where families, solo travelers, and friends can reconnect with nature and each other.



Meet Our Team

Introducing Our Team! The exceptional quality of your stay is a direct result of the passion and dedication of our team. We are more than just staff; we are a group of outdoor enthusiasts, hospitality experts, and local guides united by a common goal: to ensure you have the safest, most relaxing, and most memorable camping experience possible.



Our Values

  • Stewardship: We are committed to protecting and preserving the natural beauty of our surroundings, ensuring a sustainable and pristine environment for all.
  • Hospitality: We strive to provide attentive, genuine care, creating a safe and welcoming atmosphere where every guest feels respected and at home.
  • Authenticity: We offer a true, high-quality outdoor experience that fosters real connection with nature, relaxation, and honest, reliable service.
